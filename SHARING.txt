Sharing TC Auto Orient

Gmail may block this ZIP because it contains Windows launchers and PowerShell
installation scripts. ZIP compression does not make executable attachments
acceptable to Gmail. This source-build installer contains no precompiled DLL,
but its scripts may still trigger Gmail attachment restrictions.

Share the complete ZIP through a normal Google Drive download link:
1. Upload TC-Auto-Orient-Source-Installer.zip to your Drive.
2. Share that file with the intended recipient.
3. Send the file's Drive link in your message.
4. The recipient downloads and extracts the entire ZIP, closes Revit, and
   double-clicks Install.cmd inside the TC-Auto-Orient folder.

Google's guidance: https://support.google.com/mail/answer/6590/

The launcher uses a process-only PowerShell execution-policy override. It does
not change saved policy, disable antivirus, elevate privileges, or download build
tools. Organizational Group Policy takes precedence. If organizational policy
blocks installation, use your organization's approved software deployment process.
