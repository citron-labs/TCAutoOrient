Sharing TC Auto Orient

Upload TC-Auto-Orient-Offline-Installer.zip to Google Drive and share the download
link with the intended recipient. Gmail may block ZIP attachments containing
DLLs, CMD launchers and PowerShell scripts.

The recipient extracts the whole ZIP, closes Revit, and runs Install.cmd.
No SDK installation or source compilation is required.

Google guidance: https://support.google.com/mail/answer/6590/
